Blackjack Count Strategy Simulator

Six-deck shoe simulation with deep penetration, configurable count tags and index deviations, a 15-unit starting bankroll, +3 bet ramp, and side-by-side Hi-Lo benchmarking.

Game & simulation

Starting bankroll is fixed at 15 × table minimum. The counter bets 1 unit below +3 and 2 units at +3 or higher. You can choose whether that trigger uses running count or true count.

Important simulation notes

This is a strategy-comparison simulator, not a guarantee of casino results. It models a finite shoe and card removal, but it does not model every casino-specific rule, heat, mistakes, side bets, wonging, shuffle tracking, variable penetration, dealer procedural differences, or real-world bankroll constraints beyond stopping when the bankroll can no longer fund the next wager.
